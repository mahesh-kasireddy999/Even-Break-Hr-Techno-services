OUR SERVICES

What we deliver

01

3D BIM Modeling

Creating accurate, data-rich 3D models that assess technical and financial viability, construction stability and project performance before building begins.

02

Clash Detection & Coordination

Identifying and resolving conflicts between architectural, structural and MEP systems early, reducing field rework and costly delays.

03

Scan to BIM

Using 3D laser scanned data to create precise digital representations of existing conditions for renovation, retrofit and documentation projects.

04

Construction & Shop Drawings

Producing detailed fabrication and shop drawings with all specifications needed by contractors and fabricators for accurate on-site execution.

05

Quantity Take-Off & As-Built Models

Extracting accurate quantity take-offs from BIM models and delivering verified as-built models that reflect final construction.

06

BIM Coordination & VDC Management

Managing the full BIM coordination process across disciplines, ensuring all teams work from a single, reliable shared dataset.
